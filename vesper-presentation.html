01 · Voice Safety Gate & Frontline Challenge Full-Duplex WebRTC · 1065 ms Barge-In

Voice is the Safety Gate, Not a Transcription Box

The Premise & The Solution

Indian construction sites suffer from chronic divergence between what was tendered, what is built, and what is reported — where standard dictation tools blindly log flawed numbers under 5 dB machinery noise, cementing costly structural errors once concrete cures. Vesper transforms voice into an active operational safety gate: listening to field engineers in Hinglish, English, and Hindi, it deterministically validates spoken observations against live drawings and IS codes, verbally challenging discrepancies on site before an invalid entry ever reaches the permanent record.

Problems Faced & How We Solved Them Frontline Engineering
Problem 1 · Dictation Trap: Blind STT transcribes mistakes into permanent logs
Solved · Deterministic Safety Engine: Validates facts against SQL views before commit
Problem 2 · 5 dB Noise & Hinglish: Machinery noise breaks standard cloud speech models
Solved · Sarvam STT + Custom Normaliser: Native Indian accents & civil site terms
Problem 3 · Mid-Sentence Hesitations: Managers self-correct mid-speech, confusing turns
Solved · LiveKit WebRTC 1065 ms Barge-In: Cancels stale claims and binds live corrections
Problem 4 · Superseded Drawings: Inspecting from memory against outdated revisions
Solved · Automated Revision Grounding: Flags superseded drawing citations out loud

03 · Deterministic Safety Engine Pure Python Rules · SQLite Views · VoiceID Gate

Deterministic Rules Over SQL — Not an LLM Vibe Check

Safety decisions come from SQL views over the verified record. Zero model participation in safety verdicts.

Why Deterministic Logic Safety Principle
  • Zero LLM in the verdict: Contradictions are evaluated via SQL views: current_facts, permit_blockers, and open_hold_points.
  • No hallucination risk: The LLM is restricted to phrasing words; it never decides whether work is compliant.
  • Stops errors before rework: Cover 25 mm vs 40 ± 5 mm cannot be logged as "done" — it triggers an immediate challenge and NCR.
Speaker Authorization Gate SpeechBrain Voiceprint
  • Utterance-level verification: Verifies the speaker on the specific command utterance, not once at login.
  • Fails closed: Unenrolled voice scores 0.03 vs 0.70 threshold; writes are locked while questions remain active.
  • Audit trail: Every turn is stored alongside the drawing revision it was checked against.
The 5 Hard Safety Rules
dimension_mismatch Tolerance Check

Spoken value contradicts latest drawing fact beyond stated tolerance.

revision_mismatch Superseded Drawing

Cited revision ≠ latest For Construction revision in register.

unknown_drawing Register Check

Drawing cited does not exist in official project documentation.

permit_blocker Safety Mandatory

Mandatory safety check unsatisfied (e.g. hot work lacking assigned fire watch).

hold_point_blocker QA/QC Hold

Pre-pour hold point unreleased (e.g. pour held while cover shortfall is open).

Why Hybrid Retrieval Dense + Sparse
  • Vectors alone miss exact codes: Fails on IS 456 Cl. 26.4 or RFI-047; BM25 alone misses semantics.
  • LanceDB + SQLite FTS5: Merged via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF).
  • Cross-Encoder reranking: Cross-encoder reads query and passage together, eliminating false hits.
Receipts & Proven Safety Grounding & Results
  • Official citations: IS codes, 550 QA/QC templates, and 1,399 CPWD DSR rate items.
  • Abstention is a feature: Below threshold, explicitly says "not in the record" instead of hallucinating.
  • Project isolation: Pre-filtered datasets guarantee zero cross-project data leaks.
  • External Integrations: Exposes 11 tools over JSON-RPC for external agent workflows.
